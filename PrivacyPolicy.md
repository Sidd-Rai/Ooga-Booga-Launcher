# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** July 19, 2026

## TL;DR

I don't collect your data.

Seriously.

Ooga Booga Launcher doesn't even have the **Internet** permission, so it has nowhere to send your data even if it wanted to.

## What data do I collect?

None.

No analytics.
No trackers.
No ads.
No account.
No cloud sync.
No "anonymous usage statistics."
No AI watching how many times you open Instagram.

## What permissions does the app use?

Only the permissions required to function as an Android launcher.

It **does not** request Internet access.

## Where does my data go?

Nowhere.

Anything the launcher stores (like your preferences) stays on your device.

## Why?

Because I made this launcher for myself. I wanted something simple, private, and distraction-free and not another app trying to know everything about me.

Why would I do that? I know everything about me. That doesn't make any sense!

Also... collecting user data sounds like work, and I'm far too lazy for that.

## Changes to this Privacy Policy

If I ever decide to change how the app works in a way that affects your privacy, I'll update this page.

Though if that ever involves adding trackers, you're legally allowed to be disappointed.
