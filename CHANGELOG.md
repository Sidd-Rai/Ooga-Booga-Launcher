## 1.0.0 — 2026-07-19

Initial public release of Ooga Booga Launcher.

### Highlights

- Offline, searchable text-based app drawer
- Configurable home apps, hidden apps, and bottom shortcuts
- Android widgets across home, left, and right screens
- Movable and resizable widgets with persisted layouts
- Custom light/dark appearance and matching solid wallpaper
- Distracting-app timers with custom durations and limited extensions
- ShutUp notification filtering
- No Internet permission, analytics, advertising, or accounts
